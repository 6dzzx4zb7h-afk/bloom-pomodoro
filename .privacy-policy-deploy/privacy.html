Bloom Privacy Policy

Effective July 29, 2026

Bloom is a local-first focus timer. The Android app works without an account and has no runtime network permission.

Data stored on your device

Bloom stores the name you enter, settings, tasks, goals, timer and session records, plans, and optional Companion moments locally on your device. This information supports the app’s timer, history, and personal insights.

No collection or sharing

Bloom does not transmit this information off your device. It has no advertising, analytics, account, cloud sync, or third-party tracking SDK. Bloom does not collect, sell, or share personal or sensitive data. Google Play and the Android operating system may process store, purchase, security, or device information under their own policies; Bloom does not receive that information.

Export, import, and retention

Export creates a file only when you choose that action. Import reads only the file you select. Local data remains until you clear it in Bloom, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the app. Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup are disabled for Bloom.

Changes and contact

If a future release changes Bloom’s data practices, this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration will be updated before that release. For privacy questions, use the developer contact shown on Bloom’s Google Play listing and avoid including personal focus records in your message.
